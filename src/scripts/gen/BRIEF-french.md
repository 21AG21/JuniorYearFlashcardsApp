Read SPEC-french.md, including its "Settled by the theme 1 pilot" section; it holds the rubric rows and task formats. In short:
- Topics are the theme's recommended contexts from the skeleton, copied with their slug codes and French titles; topic `s` is a skill code (1.B).
- Keep every existing vocabulary card verbatim (TRANSLATE, French to English, the example sentence and accents exact), placed in the context it fits. Correct any wrong existing note and say so.
- Add cultural cards on the products, practices and perspectives of the Francophone world under each context (France, Québec, Belgium, Switzerland, the Maghreb, West and Central Africa, the Caribbean, Louisiana), and reading and listening moves on short French texts you write. Tag vocabulary 2.A/2.A.2, culture 3.A/3.A.1–3.A.3, reading 1.A–1.C, connectors 2.D/2.D.2, register 2.A/2.A.4.
- 40 to 50 explain-whys in French ("Expliquez pourquoi …"), the chain in `h`, a model paragraph of at most 320 characters, other valid arguments in `o`, weak answers in `w` as a student writes them (no example, no connector, wrong register, an English calque).
- Hedge any figure you are not sure of; label every text you compose as invented.
